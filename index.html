How to install
  1. Open this page in Safari (it will not work from inside other apps or browsers).
  2. Tap Install Cherify above, then tap Install on the popup.
  3. Close Safari and check your home screen. The app icon appears and downloads in the background (1 to 2 minutes).
If the app does not open and you see "Untrusted Developer": go to Settings > General > VPN & Device Management, tap "cherify llc" and tap Trust.

This build only installs on devices registered with Cherify. If installation fails, contact us so we can register your device.
